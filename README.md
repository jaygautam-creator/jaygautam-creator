### What I'm building now

**RampBrain** — a company brain for AI agents. It compiles how a team actually works — docs, code, and people's heads — into grounded, governed skills an agent runs over MCP: refunds, incidents, approvals, access. Every step is traced to a source and every action waits for human approval. → **[rampbrain.com](https://rampbrain.com)**

**Aletheia** — an evidence-grounded, multi-agent verification framework that checks whether an LLM's claim is actually supported by the underlying literature, instead of trusting its confident tone. Ships with a real evaluation harness. → **[live demo](https://aletheia-kappa-one.vercel.app)** · **[repo](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/Aletheia)**

**River** — a continuous, memory-aware AI companion that keeps the thread across conversations without taking control of it: memories are consent-based, visible, editable, and reversible. → **[live](https://river-sigma-three.vercel.app)** · **[repo](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/river)**

**Options paper-trading experiment** — a pre-registered, defined-risk strategy on Indian index options, running fully automated with a strict evidence gate before any real capital. An exercise in disciplined automation, not a tip sheet.

*RampBrain and the trading system are private for now — happy to give a walkthrough.*

### Selected work

**[Dobara](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/Dobara)** · `Python` · `LightGBM` · `Next.js`<br>An AI revenue-recovery agent for Indian recurring payments. It treats every retry as a priced bet against the customer cancelling their mandate, and stops when the trade looks bad. Razorpay AI Buildathon, Track 03. → [live demo](https://dobara-one.vercel.app)

**[Support Triage Agent](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/hiver-support-agent)** · `Python` · `Gemini` · `Evaluation`<br>A first-response agent for @AppleSupport built from 2.8M tweets: intent, grounded reply, and an escalate-or-handle decision citing a policy clause. The evaluation is the point — a blind control, a second annotator, and ablations that each cost the headline something.

**[DealFlow360](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/dealflow360)** · `TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `PostgreSQL`<br>A self-governing B2B sales-ops platform: discount governance, approval routing that fails closed, multi-warehouse fulfilment, and hybrid billing. Odoo Hackathon 2026 Grand Finale, 24-hour solo build.

**[Dyla](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/dyla-stump-the-model)** · `Python` · `CLIP` · `FAISS`<br>Visual search for jewellery — photograph a piece and find its closest matches among 7,000+ catalogue photos, tested on a hand-shot set of hard real-world phone photos. → [live demo](https://frontend-neon-nine-bl3oyccpzz.vercel.app)

**[CuraLens](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/CuraLens)** · `Python` · `Deep Learning` · `Computer Vision`<br>AI-assisted oral-cancer screening — image analysis and risk scoring meant to support clinicians at the point of care.

**[Blackout Protocol](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/Blackout-Protocol)** · `Kotlin` · `Android` · `Mesh`<br>An offline SOS mesh network for disasters. When the towers go down, messages hop phone-to-phone until one device reaches the internet and syncs.

**[Offline UPI Wallet](https://github.com/jaygautam-creator/offline-upi-wallet)** · `Android` · `NFC` · `QR`<br>UPI-style payments with no signal required — NFC, QR, and audio transfer over a local ledger that auto-syncs once you're back online.
